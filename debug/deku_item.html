Description

Race to save the world from a Maya apocalypse. Lara must venture through deadly and exotic locations, surviving the unforgiving jungle and braving terrifying tombs to reach the legendary lost Inca city of Paititi. Using advanced stealth techniques and an arsenal of weapons, Lara has to stop the shadowy organization, Trinity, from stealing the power of an ancient god before it’s too late. Dive into Lara’s past while racing to save the future in this incredible final chapter of the Tomb Raider reboot, rebuilt for Nintendo Switch™ 2.

Explore beyond the story with Lara: raid deadly Challenge Tombs to unlock rewards, scavenge resources, craft gear, and complete side-missions.

Shadow of the Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition includes the base game, all seven DLC challenge tombs, 2-player online co-op, as well as all downloadable weapons, outfits, and skills.
