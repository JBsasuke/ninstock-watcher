Description

In Prinny Party: Going Overboard!, you and your friends will do whatever it takes to defeat the Supreme Overboard and his Protago-Knights! Move around the board, earn experience, acquire more powerful equipment, and turn in quests to stay ahead of the curve. Then, when a boss spawns, work together to take him down!

Or stab each other in the back and enjoy the chaos. Prinny Party: Going Overboard! – Nintendo Switch™ 2 Edition features improved resolution and GameShare functionality, meaning you can play with your friends anywhere around the world with just one copy of the game!
